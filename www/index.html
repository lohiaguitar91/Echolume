How to sing

Tap the water. Your song carries you there and lights the cave as it goes.

Songs are loud. Hunters swim toward the last place you sang.

Hold, then let go, to throw your voice. The song lands where you held — it moves you nowhere, and everything listening goes there instead of here.

Gather the amber motes: each one brightens your glow. The vent below, ringed and green, takes you deeper.

Every tap is one song. Stars want three things: the vent reached, the motes gathered, few songs spent.

Clear depth 7 to open the Abyss: an endless dive where depth is your score and the deep never stops narrowing.

About

Echolume is a game about a small blind creature of the deep that moves the only way it knows: singing into the dark and riding its own echo.

Every pulse of song pushes you through the water and lights the world for a moment. But the deep listens back. Sing carefully.
