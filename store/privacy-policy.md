# Echolume — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 16, 2026

Echolume is a free game supported by advertising. It has no accounts, no
telemetry of its own, and no analytics. This page explains the one exception:
the ads.

## The short version

Echolume itself does not collect anything about you. Your progress and settings
live on your device and never leave it. The game does show ads, and the Google
AdMob software that serves them does collect data, described below. You can turn
ads off permanently with a one-time purchase.

## Advertising

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Ads appear in two places only: after you clear
certain depths, and as an optional "watch to revive" you may choose at a boss.
Ads never interrupt a failed run.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following, as described in
[Google's data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure):

- **IP address**, which may be used to estimate a device's general location
- **Crash logs** and **performance data** from the ad software
- **Device identifiers**, including the advertising identifier
- **Advertising data**, meaning a record of ads shown
- **User interactions** with ads, such as taps and video views

Google processes this data as an independent controller under its own
[Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Advertising terms](https://business.safety.google/adsservices/). Echolume does
not receive, store, or have access to this data, and never combines it with
anything else.

### Your choices

- **On iOS**, the app asks for permission before any tracking. If you decline,
  ads still appear but are not personalised using your advertising identifier.
  You can change this any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.
- **On Android**, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in
  **Settings → Google → Ads**.
- **On any platform**, the one-time **Remove Ads** purchase stops interstitial
  ads permanently. The optional revive stays available if you want it.

## In-app purchase

Echolume offers a single non-consumable purchase, **Remove Ads**. The
transaction is handled entirely by Apple or Google. Echolume never sees your
payment details, and stores only a local flag recording that you bought it.

## Optional Game Center / Google Play Games

If you choose to sign in to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, Echolume
submits your Abyss depth to a leaderboard and unlocks achievements through that
service. This is processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies;
Echolume receives nothing back and stores nothing about your platform account.
Declining changes nothing except that milestones stay on your device only.

## Data stored on your device

Your progress (depths cleared, stars, banked light, best Abyss depth) and your
settings (sound, music, haptics, accessibility toggles, whether you removed ads)
are saved locally. This never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it,
and you can erase it any time via **Settings → Reset progress**.

## Children's privacy

Echolume is suitable for all ages, and Echolume itself collects nothing from
anyone. Because the game does serve advertising, we do not direct it at children
under 13, and it is not listed in a children's category on either store. If you
believe a child has been served personalised advertising through this app,
contact us at the address below and we will help you reset the identifier.

## Permissions

Echolume requests no sensitive permissions. It does not access your location,
contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files. On iOS it asks once for tracking
permission, which you are free to decline.

## Changes to this policy

If a future update changes what the app does with data, this policy will be
updated and the change described in the release notes before it takes effect.
