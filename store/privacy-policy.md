# Echolume — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 8, 2026

Echolume is a premium offline game. It is built to collect nothing.

## Data we collect

**None.** Echolume does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or usage data. Specifically, the app has:

- No analytics or telemetry of any kind
- No advertising and no ad SDKs
- No user accounts and no sign-in
- No third-party SDKs that process data
- No network requests: the game runs entirely on your device

## Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, best Abyss depth) and your settings (sound, music, haptics, accessibility toggles) are saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time inside the app via **Settings → Reset progress**.

## Children's privacy

Echolume is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

## Permissions

Echolume requests no sensitive permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

## Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes what the app does with data, this policy will be updated and the change will be described in the release notes before it takes effect.
